# Time and work

## Formulas
- One day's work = 1 / days
- Together: add rates; pipes that empty subtract
- Two workers: together = ab / (a + b) days

## Shortcuts
- Use the LCM of the days as total work. Rates become whole numbers and fractions disappear.
- The ab/(a+b) shortcut works only for exactly two workers.

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **A takes 12 days, B takes 18. Together?** (12 × 18)/(12 + 18) = 216/30 = **7.2 days**.
2. **A takes 10 days, B takes 15. They work together for 3 days, then A leaves. How long does B take to finish?** Done: 3 × (1/10 + 1/15) = 1/2. Left: 1/2 × 15 = **7.5 days**.
3. **Pipes fill a tank in 20 h and 30 h; a drain empties it in 60 h. All open?** 1/20 + 1/30 − 1/60 = 4/60, so **15 hours**.

## Traps
- Add rates, never days.
- "Leaves after 3 days" means both worked for those 3 days.
- A drain subtracts.

## Good wrong options
Average of the days; sum of the days; adding the drain instead of subtracting.
