# Time, speed and distance

## Formulas
- Distance = speed × time
- km/h to m/s: × 5/18. m/s to km/h: × 18/5
- Same distance at speeds a and b: average speed = 2ab / (a + b)
- Train crossing a bridge or platform: distance = train length + bridge length
- Relative speed: opposite directions add, same direction subtract
- Boats: still water = (down + up)/2, stream = (down − up)/2

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **54 km/h in m/s:** 54 × 5/18 = **15 m/s**.
2. **Same distance at 40 and 60 km/h. Average speed?** 2 × 40 × 60 / 100 = **48 km/h**.
3. **Train 200 m at 72 km/h crosses a 300 m bridge. Time?** 72 km/h = 20 m/s; 500/20 = **25 s**.
4. **Boat goes 15 km/h downstream and 9 km/h upstream:** still water **12**, stream **3**.

## Traps
- Average speed is not (a + b)/2 unless the times are equal.
- Convert units before dividing.
- Add the train's own length.
- 2ab/(a+b) needs equal distances.

## Good wrong options
Simple average (50 in example 2); leaving out the train's length; no unit conversion.
