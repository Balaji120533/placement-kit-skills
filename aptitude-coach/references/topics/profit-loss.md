# Profit and loss

## Formulas
- Profit % = (SP − CP) / CP × 100. Loss % uses the same base, CP.
- SP = CP × (100 + profit%) / 100
- Discount is on the marked price (MP): SP = MP × (100 − d%) / 100
- Successive discounts: multiply the factors
- Same SP, x% profit on one and x% loss on the other: overall loss = x²/100 %
- False weight: profit % = (error / weight used) × 100

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **CP 400, SP 460:** 60/400 × 100 = **15% profit**.
2. **MP 1000, discounts 20% then 10%:** 1000 × 0.8 × 0.9 = **720**.
3. **Two articles sold at 990 each, 10% profit on one and 10% loss on the other:** 10²/100 = **1% loss**. Check: CPs are 900 and 1100, total 2000; SP total 1980; loss 20.
4. **Dealer sells at cost price but uses 900 g for 1 kg:** 100/900 × 100 = **11.11% profit**.

## Traps
- Profit % is on CP; discount % is on MP.
- Successive discounts do not add: 20% + 10% is 28%, not 30%.
- Equal profit and loss percentages on the same SP do not cancel out.
- False weight divides by the weight actually given (900), not 1000.

## Good wrong options
Use SP as the base; add discounts; say "no profit no loss"; divide by 1000 in false weight.
