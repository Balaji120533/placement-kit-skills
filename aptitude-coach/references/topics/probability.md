# Probability

## Formulas
- P(event) = favourable / total equally likely outcomes
- P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
- Independent events: multiply
- Without replacement: the second fraction changes

## Shortcuts
- "At least one": 1 − P(none).
- Two dice give 36 outcomes; a sum of 7 has the most ways (6).

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **Two dice, sum 7:** 6/36 = **1/6**.
2. **Bag of 3 red and 5 blue, draw 2 without replacement, both red:** 3/8 × 2/7 = **3/28**.
3. **3 coin tosses, at least one head:** 1 − 1/8 = **7/8**.

## Traps
- Forgetting that both the numerator and denominator drop without replacement.
- Treating (1,6) and (6,1) as one outcome.
- Adding probabilities for "and".

## Good wrong options
With-replacement answer (9/64 in example 2); 3/8 for example 3 ("exactly one"); counting pairs as unordered.
