# Averages

## Formulas
- Average = sum / count, so sum = average × count
- Combined average = (n₁a₁ + n₂a₂) / (n₁ + n₂)
- Evenly spaced numbers: average = middle term = (first + last) / 2

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **Average of 5 numbers is 24; a sixth makes it 26. Find the sixth:** 26 × 6 − 24 × 5 = 156 − 120 = **36**.
2. **Class of 30 averages 60, class of 20 averages 80. Combined average?** (1800 + 1600)/50 = **68**.
3. **Average of 11 consecutive odd numbers starting at 5:** the middle (6th) term is 5 + 10 = **15**.

## Traps
- Do not average two averages when group sizes differ: (60 + 80)/2 = 70 is wrong above.
- Convert to sums first when a number is added, removed or replaced.
- Average speed is not this formula; see `time-speed-distance.md`.

## Good wrong options
The simple average of averages; forgetting to change the count; the middle term off by one.
