# Clocks

## Formulas
- Hour hand moves 0.5° per minute; minute hand moves 6° per minute.
- Angle at H:M = |30H − 5.5M| (if above 180°, subtract from 360°)
- The hands coincide 11 times in 12 hours, 22 times in a day.

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **Angle at 3:15:** |90 − 82.5| = **7.5°**.
2. **Angle at 4:20:** |120 − 110| = **10°**.
3. **When do the hands coincide between 2 and 3?** 30 × 2 = 5.5M, so M = 120/11 = **10 10/11 minutes past 2**.

## Traps
- At 3:15 the hour hand has moved past 3, so the angle is not 0.
- The hands coincide 11 times in 12 hours, not 12.
- Give the smaller angle unless the question says otherwise.

## Good wrong options
0° for 3:15; 12 or 24 coincidences; forgetting the hour hand's movement.
