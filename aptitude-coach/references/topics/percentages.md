# Percentages

## Formulas
- x% of y = xy / 100
- Percentage change = (change / original) × 100
- Successive changes a% and b%: net = a + b + ab/100 (use negatives for decreases)
- Price up r%, spend unchanged: cut consumption by r / (100 + r) × 100%

## Shortcuts
- Build from 10%, 5% and 1%: 15% of 240 = 24 + 12 = 36.
- x% of y = y% of x, so 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4.

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **15% of 240:** 10% = 24, 5% = 12, total **36**.
2. **+20% then −10%:** 20 − 10 + (20 × −10)/100 = **+8%**.
3. **Price up 25%, how much to cut consumption to spend the same?** 25/125 × 100 = **20%**.

## Traps
- Changes do not simply add: +20% then −20% is −4%, not 0.
- The base changes between successive steps.
- "A is 25% more than B" does not mean "B is 25% less than A" (B is 20% less).

## Good wrong options
Add the percentages directly; use the new value as the base; forget the ab/100 term.
