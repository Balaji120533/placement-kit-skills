# Blood relations

## Method
Draw a family tree: + for male, − for female, a horizontal line for siblings or spouses, a vertical line for parent and child. Read the relation from the tree, and work from the end of a long sentence backwards.

## Solved examples
1. **Pointing at a man, Ravi says, "He is the son of my paternal grandfather's only son."** Paternal grandfather's only son = Ravi's father. The man is his father's son: **Ravi's brother**.
2. **Code: A + B means A is the father of B; A × B means A is the brother of B. What is P + Q × R?** P is Q's father and Q is R's brother, so **P is R's father**.
3. **A is B's sister. B is C's brother. How is C related to A?** A is C's sister, but C's gender is unknown: **cannot be determined** (brother or sister).

## Traps
- A name is not proof of gender. Use only what the question states.
- "Sibling" and "child" give no gender.
- "Grandfather's only son" needs the word paternal; a maternal grandfather's only son is an uncle.
- "C is D's son" does not say whether D is the father or the mother.

## When generating
State the gender of anyone whose gender the answer depends on. List every reading of the sentence; if two answers survive, fix the wording or make "cannot be determined" the answer.
