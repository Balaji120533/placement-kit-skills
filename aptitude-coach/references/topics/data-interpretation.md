# Data interpretation

## Method
Read the table's title, units and labels first. Then read the question, and calculate only what it asks. Round late, and estimate when the options are far apart.

## Formulas
- Percentage change = (new − old) / old × 100
- Share of total = part / total × 100
- Average = total / count

## Solved examples (verified)

Sales in units:

| Company | 2023 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 200 | 250 |
| B | 300 | 330 |
| C | 500 | 420 |

1. **Percentage rise for A:** 50/200 × 100 = **25%**.
2. **Change in total sales:** 1000 to 1000: **0%**.
3. **A in 2024 to C in 2023:** 250:500 = **1:2**.
4. **B's share of 2024 sales:** 330/1000 × 100 = **33%**.

## Traps
- Using the new value as the base for percentage change.
- Mixing up the years in a row or column.
- Units in thousands or lakhs.

## Good wrong options
20% (50/250, the wrong base); values from the wrong year; a ratio written the wrong way round.

## When generating
Show the data as a compact table, 3 to 5 rows. Compute every answer from it with code. Several questions may share one table.
