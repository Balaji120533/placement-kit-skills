# Direction sense

## Method
Draw it, with north at the top. Track east and north as net totals. Straight-line distance = √(east² + north²).

Turning right moves N → E → S → W. Turning left goes the other way.

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **Walk 3 m north, then 4 m east. Distance and direction from the start?** √(9 + 16) = **5 m, north-east**.
2. **Facing north: turn right, right, then left. Now facing?** N → E → S → **East**.
3. **Walk 10 m south, 5 m east, 10 m north, 3 m west. Where are you?** North-south cancels; east total 5 − 3: **2 m east of the start**.

## Traps
- Distance walked is not distance from the start.
- Right and left depend on the way you are facing, not on the page.
- Shadows: the morning sun is in the east, so shadows fall west; evening is the reverse.

## Good wrong options
Total distance walked (7 m in example 1); the opposite direction; mixing up the last turn.
