# Mensuration

## Formulas
- Circle: area πr², circumference 2πr
- Right triangle: area ½ × base × height; hypotenuse² = base² + height²
- Cube of edge a: volume a³, surface area 6a²
- Cylinder: volume πr²h, curved surface 2πrh
- Cone: volume ⅓πr²h
- Sphere: volume (4/3)πr³, surface area 4πr²

## Shortcuts
- Use π = 22/7 when the radius is a multiple of 7.
- Scaling: lengths × k means area × k² and volume × k³.
- Percentage change in area: use the successive formula a + b + ab/100.

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **Circle, r = 7:** area **154**, circumference **44**.
2. **Cylinder, r = 7, h = 10:** volume **1540**, curved surface **440**.
3. **Edge of a cube doubles. Volume?** 2³: **8 times**.
4. **Length +20%, breadth −10%. Area?** 20 − 10 − 2 = **+8%**.
5. **Right triangle with legs 6 and 8:** area **24**, hypotenuse **10**.
6. **Sphere, r = 21:** (4/3) × (22/7) × 9261 = **38808**.

## Traps
- Diameter given when the formula needs the radius.
- Mixed units (cm and m).
- Curved surface vs total surface.

## Good wrong options
2 times for a doubled cube edge; using the diameter as the radius; total surface in place of curved surface.
