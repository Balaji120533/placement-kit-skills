# Number and letter series

## Method
1. Take differences. Then take differences of the differences.
2. Try ratios, squares and cubes, n(n+1), and alternating sequences.
3. For letters, convert to positions (A = 1, Z = 26).

## Solved examples (verified)
1. **2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?** Differences 4, 6, 8, 10, so next is 12, or n(n+1): **42**.
2. **3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ?** Differences double: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32: **65**.
3. **A, C, F, J, O, ?** Gaps 2, 3, 4, 5, then 6: 15 + 6 = 21: **U**.

## Traps
- Alternating series: check odd and even positions separately.
- Stopping at the first rule that fits only some terms.
- Letter positions wrap past Z only if the question says so.

## When generating
Give at least 5 terms so the rule is clear. Check that no other simple rule leads to a different option.
